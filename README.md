## Project "Detecting Abnormalities in Electronic Circuit Boards on a Conveyor Belt."

This project is designed to develop an advanced system, leveraging computer vision and machine learning, in manufacturing environment for detecting faults and anomalies on printed circuit boards (PCBs) as they move along a conveyor belt. 
When a defective product is identified, the system will provide detailed information about the specific location of each fault on the board. Our goal is to enhance quality control, minimize defective products, and optimize production efficiency through automated monitoring.
